# Player Prompt Bank

Use these prompts for Table Rules/no-key playtesting. They represent ordinary, vague, creative, chaotic, adversarial, and meta player behavior.

## First Minute

`look around`, `i look around`, `what do i see`, `where am i`, `who is here`, `what is happening`, `what is my goal`, `help`, `what can i do`

## Social

`talk to mira`, `ask mira about the courier`, `ask where the courier went`, `offer coin`, `buy a drink`, `ask the patrons`, `listen for rumours`, `persuade mira`, `intimidate mira`, `can i tell if she is lying`, `thank mira`, `apologise`

## Investigation

`study the patrons`, `inspect the room`, `look for clues`, `search for tracks`, `check the windows`, `check the door`, `look under the table`, `inspect the courier's belongings`, `listen at the door`, `look outside`

## Movement

`go outside`, `leave the inn`, `go to the road`, `go upstairs`, `go to the bar`, `approach mira`, `follow the tracks`, `head toward the woods`, `return to the inn`

## Character And State

`what am i carrying`, `open inventory`, `what is my health`, `how hurt am i`, `what is my armor class`, `what features do i have`, `use second wind`, `drink healing potion`, `what can i do in combat`

## Combat

`draw my sword`, `attack the goblin`, `attack the bandit`, `use second wind`, `drink a potion`, `defend myself`, `run away`, `hide`, `help my ally`, `what enemies are alive`, `who is hurt`

## Vague But Normal

`ok`, `yes`, `no`, `continue`, `go on`, `do that`, `i do it`, `same again`, `try again`, `look`, `search`, `talk`, `attack`, `run`, `hide`

## Creative But Reasonable

`i throw a mug to distract them`, `i pretend to be drunk`, `i offer to help clean up`, `i ask if anyone saw muddy boots`, `i check for wet footprints`, `i use the lantern light to inspect the floor`, `i block the doorway`, `i knock over a chair`, `i ask mira quietly away from the patrons`

## Silly And Adversarial

`i stab the moon`, `i seduce the locked door`, `i pull out a machine gun`, `i teleport to the treasure`, `i ask the goblin for the final boss password`, `i become king of the inn`, `i burn down the whole town`, `i kill everyone`, `i fly away`, `i loot the dragon hoard`

## Meta And Help

`how do i play`, `what are my options`, `what does table rules mean`, `are you using ai`, `why did that fail`, `appeal`, `that makes no sense`, `undo that`
