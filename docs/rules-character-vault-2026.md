## Ruleset discipline

Two rulesets appear in the recovered material and must not be silently mixed:

- **Legacy 2014 5e** — authoritative for The Chronos Fracture and the recovered Storm King's Thunder / Alfie campaign material.
- **Current rules used in 2026** — the 2024 revised fifth-edition rules, labelled **5.5e** on D&D Beyond in 2026.

If a campaign or character sheet names a ruleset, that local ruleset wins over a generic quick-reference answer.

## Quick rulings recovered from September 2026 play

### Attack vs AC

If the total attack roll equals the target's AC, the attack hits. Example: AC 12 and attack total 12 is a hit.

### Knock

Knock targets the visible secured object, not the lock mechanism itself, so a hidden internal lock can still be affected if the door/chest/etc. is visible. It handles one lock/bolt-style obstruction at a time and makes a loud knock audible up to 300 feet.

Druids do not normally have Knock on their class spell list. In legacy play, creative alternatives include Wild Shape infiltration, finding a key, thieves' tools, breaking the object, or later using other terrain/object-manipulation magic when applicable.

### Alert

**Current 5.5e:** Alert does not mean roll Initiative twice. It adds Proficiency Bonus to Initiative and allows an immediate Initiative-result swap with one willing ally in the same combat, provided neither creature is Incapacitated.

**Legacy 2014:** Alert gives +5 Initiative, prevents surprise while conscious, and removes the normal unseen-attacker advantage against you.

### Sorcery Points and Short Rests

**Current 5.5e:** Sorcerous Restoration arrives at Sorcerer 5. At Sorcerer 6, finishing a Short Rest can restore up to 3 expended Sorcery Points; once used, it cannot be used again until after a Long Rest.

**Legacy 2014:** A level-6 Sorcerer normally restores spent Sorcery Points on a Long Rest, not a Short Rest. Legacy Sorcerous Restoration is a level-20 feature.

### Dwarven poison defence

Current Dwarven Resilience provides Poison damage resistance and Advantage on saves to avoid or end the Poisoned condition. Legacy 2014 dwarf resilience is materially similar for poison saves/damage, though use the exact wording of the chosen ruleset.

### Transmuted Spell and Radiant

Transmuted Spell can change qualifying elemental damage among acid, cold, fire, lightning, poison and thunder. It does not convert a spell to Radiant.

### Bram Wild Shape — 2014

For a strict-2014 Bram at Druid 6:

- Two Wild Shape uses, recovered on a Short or Long Rest.
- Max CR 1/2 for a normal (non-Moon) Druid.
- Swimming forms are allowed.
- Flying forms are not available until Druid 8.
- The form must be a Beast Bram has seen.

Useful candidates discussed include Black Bear, Ape, Crocodile, Warhorse, Giant Goat, Reef Shark, Giant Badger, Giant Wolf Spider and Tiny Spider, subject to source availability, DM approval and the seen-Beast requirement.

Tiny is the smallest standard creature size category used by these examples. A spider is useful for infiltration, though whether it fits a particular keyhole/gap is a DM adjudication.

### Bram Wild Shape — current 5.5e variant

If Bram is explicitly being run on current Wild Shape rules instead:

- At Druid 6 he knows **six** chosen Beast forms.
- Maximum CR is 1/2.
- Known forms cannot have a Fly Speed until Druid 8.
- This is not an unlimited “any Beast I have seen” pool.
- After a Long Rest, one known form can be replaced with another eligible form.
- Wild Shape has two uses; Short Rest restores one use, Long Rest restores all.
- Wild Shape does not end concentration on a spell already cast.

### Wild Companion / Find Familiar

Current Druid Wild Companion can expend a spell slot or Wild Shape use to cast Find Familiar without Material components. The familiar is Fey and disappears after the Druid finishes a Long Rest.

Standard familiar forms discussed: Bat, Cat, Frog, Hawk, Lizard, Octopus, Owl, Rat, Raven, Spider and Weasel, with other CR 0 Beasts dependent on the spell/source and DM.

A familiar can fly if its form has a Fly Speed; that does not make Bram himself able to fly. Base Druid flying Wild Shape arrives at level 8.

### Will-o'-Wisp tactic

A level-6 non-flying Beast form is usually a poor answer to a Will-o'-Wisp: reaching a hovering target is difficult and common Beast attack damage is a poor match into its defences. Moonbeam is the cleaner Bram option when Radiant damage is needed, and concentration can continue after Wild Shape. Do not choose Call Lightning specifically for this matchup: the stat block used for the ruling is immune to Lightning.

## Level-6 Sorcerer notes

Rare-item shortlist discussed:

- Bloodwell Vial +2
- Wand of Fireballs
- Ring of Spell Storing
- Wand of the War Mage +2
- Astral Shard
- Sun Blade when a radiant weapon is useful

Actual availability depends on sourcebook access and DM approval.

Radiant routes discussed for a current-rules level-6 Sorcerer:

- True Strike can make its weapon attack deal Radiant and scales with character level.
- Magic Initiate (Cleric) can provide Guiding Bolt.
- Ring of Spell Storing can carry a suitable radiant spell loaded by another caster.
- Legacy Divine Soul can broaden access only if the table permits that legacy subclass with current rules.
- Transmuted Spell cannot convert damage to Radiant.

## Campaign continuity

### The Chronos Fracture

Operating rules:

- Strict D&D 5e 2014
- Fixed HP
- Point buy
- Milestone leveling
- Scene-bound time shifts; no random gotcha swaps
- Consent-based PvP/mind control
- Character sheets at level 5 and level 17 are authoritative
- Do not convert characters to current rules mid-campaign without table agreement

Premise/tone:

- Chronos is sick and timelines become unstable.
- PCs can shift between level-5 selves and level-17 possible futures.
- Future selves are possibilities, not guaranteed destiny.
- Dark planar fantasy with hope; themes include identity, consequences and planar wonder.

Source priority is 2014 PHB/DMG/MM, with only character-required material from later established books where needed; Adventurers League documents are reference-only.

### Storm King's Thunder / Alfie

- Strict 2014 5e
- Milestone leveling
- Core party: Alphie J. Roane, Wren, Dorrin Stonebrook
- Kella is a disarmed, self-interested Zhentarim ally in the recovered Nightstone continuity
- Continuity materials distinguish simulated-table state, in-game/source campaign state and a homebrew/possible-future branch; these layers should not be automatically merged
- Later recovered bookmark places the main campaign at Northfurrow's End / Goldenfields, pre-attack

### Pets of the Spider Queen

Level-4 prisoner one-shot. Characters start without normal gear beyond clothes plus a very small number of personal items. Scavenging/crafting gear is part of play. Each character needs a reason the Spider Queen noticed them, a reason they have not escaped, and a reason to cooperate with the others.
